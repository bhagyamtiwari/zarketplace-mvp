## 1. Who you are buying from

zarketplace is the **seller and merchant of record** for everything on this
site. When you buy, your contract is with zarketplace and with nobody else.

Every item is bought by zarketplace, received at its premises, checked against
its listing, repacked and dispatched by zarketplace under its own GST
registration. Items are pre-owned; that is the nature of what is sold here.

## 2. Price and payment

**2.1** Prices are inclusive of applicable taxes and are shown in rupees.

**2.2** Payment is taken by zarketplace. You receive a zarketplace tax invoice.

**2.3** A price is what it says at the moment you check out.

## 3. Condition

**3.1** Items are pre-owned and sold as described. The listing states condition
and discloses known flaws.

**3.2** zarketplace checks each item against its listing before dispatch.
**zarketplace does not authenticate items** and makes no representation that it
has done so. If an item is materially not as described, §5 applies.

*(Open for counsel: whether §3.2 is compatible with zarketplace being the
merchant of record under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, and how it interacts
with the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules for pre-owned goods.)*

## 4. Delivery

**4.1** Items are dispatched from zarketplace's own premises, normally the same
day an item is ready.

**4.2** Tracking is provided on every order.

**4.3** Risk passes to you on delivery.

## 5. If something is wrong

**5.1** If an item arrives materially not as described, or is the wrong item,
contact zarketplace within **48 hours** of delivery with the order number and
photographs.

**5.2** zarketplace resolves the matter directly. Refunds are made to the
original payment method. See the Refund Policy.

**5.3** If an item you ordered becomes unavailable before it is dispatched, the
order is cancelled and refunded in full.

## 6. Grievances

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, the following are
published on the site:

- the identity and registered address of the seller entity
- the Grievance Officer's name, contact address and email
- acknowledgement of a grievance within 48 hours and resolution within one
  month of receipt
- the returns, refunds and cancellation policy

*(To complete before publication: registered entity name, CIN, registered
address, GSTIN, Grievance Officer name and contact.)*

## 7. Limits

Nothing here excludes any liability that cannot be excluded under Indian law,
including under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

## 8. Governing law

These terms are governed by Indian law. *(Open for counsel: jurisdiction
clause, and whether an exclusive-jurisdiction clause is enforceable against a
consumer.)*

## Notes for counsel

   and it should be stress-tested against how the site actually operates.

   That combination is the most likely point of consumer-law friction.
3. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020: confirm which obligations
   attach to a **seller** rather than a **marketplace entity**, since
   zarketplace is asserting the former.
